## Usage Guidelines

- Do not alter logo colors, proportions, or letterforms.
- Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "W" mark on all sides.
- Use the dark variant on dark/colored backgrounds; use the light variant on white/light backgrounds.
- Do not use the logo to imply partnership or endorsement without prior written consent.
